# Ports of two files by Levent Alpöge

`GroundTheory/AutomorphicForms/HilbertSchmidt.lean` and `GroundTheory/AutomorphicForms/TraceClass.lean`
are ports to Lean 4.29.0 / Mathlib v4.29.0 of the files with the same paths in the Lean archive
published by Levent Alpöge with https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf (20 November 2026; sha256 of the joined
archive `9631b4f020199e580ba86178cb56c6e9b09f690c2369a80dc4334aa93f49c7fc`; the archive is written for
Lean 4.33.1).  All credit for their content belongs to their author.

- They are NOT the originals.  Each file states in its header the sha256 of the original file and
  every change made for the port (no statement of a theorem or definition is changed).
- The archive carries no licence file and its author has not been asked.  The files are kept here,
  with attribution, only because `lean/CorrelationsToGeometryHeatOperator.lean` imports them.  They
  will be removed on the author's request.
- Nothing here says anything about the claim the archive was published for.
